I’m accessibility lead at Superhuman (formerly known as Grammarly). Most of what shows up here is about what happens when you treat design as care: for the people using the software, for the engineers building it, and for the systems that hold it all together.

For years my online presence was rented space, social profiles and digital CVs on platforms I didn’t own. This site is me building a home on the internet instead. The foundation is my own, the creaky floorboards are ones I coded, and the front door is always open.

It’s a place to slow down. A few of the longer rooms (essays, projects, the shelves) are still being arranged, but the notes are already here.
