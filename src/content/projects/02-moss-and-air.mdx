## Premise

A pseudonym for a long, slow project, a series of instrumental records made mostly outside of computers. The brief: write less, listen more, leave the noise of the day on the porch.

## What it is

- An ambient / drone project recorded to four-track cassette
- Eurorack and a Critter & Guitari Organelle for the bones of most pieces
- Field recordings collected on walks (a stream in March, a kettle in October)
- A small kalimba modded with felt, recorded one room over

## Where it's going

Two EPs a year, distributed on Bandcamp. Liner notes here when each is done.
